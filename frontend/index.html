Smart Plate is a hybrid vision-sensor system that combines deep learning-based food segmentation & recognition with NIR spectroscopy for comprehensive nutrient and oil/fat content analysis.

Upload a plate image → AI segments & classifies each food item → nutrition is looked up from our database → NIR sensor estimates oil/fat content.
